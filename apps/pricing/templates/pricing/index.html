Pricing

We have various tiers of experience for our users, ranging from a starter tier to an ultimate all-in-one tier.

  • Starter: Your current plan. Features: Basic, boring, and that's about it.
  • Premium: Starts at $9.99/month. Features: Bragging rights, "thanks for the free money!"
  • Ultimate: Starts at $49.99/month. Features: Complementary "fell for an internet scam" badge

No, we're just kidding. ;)
How diabolical would it be to have pricing before user accounts, anyway? Would that even be possible?

For now, there are no plans to create paid plans on Multiplex64, they wouldn't facilitate anything useful given our current services.
The only thing we are considering is trying out (hopefully unintrusive) ads in a far future, although current traffic is low and there's not much incentive for that either.
Maybe an ad or two on the bottom or side of the screen, to reap a whopping $0.001 per page view, or something.
